Data-Driven Analysis of Video Game Commercial Performance (2004-2008)

📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes a dataset of over 1,200 video games from the 7th console generation to identify what makes a game commercially successful. By examining review scores, platform choices, and branding (sequels), I provide insights into the economic drivers of the gaming industry during this era.

🛠️ Technologies
Language: Python 3

Libraries: Pandas (Data Manipulation), Matplotlib & Seaborn (Data Visualization)

Dataset Source: CORGIS Dataset Project

📊 Key Insights & Visualizations
1. Quality vs. Sales
There is a clear positive correlation between critical reception and sales. However, the data shows significant "outliers"—games that sold millions despite low scores, often due to strong movie licensing or marketing.

2. The Power of Sequels
Sequels consistently outperform new Intellectual Properties (IPs) in terms of average sales. This highlights the industry's reliance on established fanbases to minimize financial risk.

3. Genre Market Mapping
Using bubble chart analysis, I categorized genres by their "niche" vs. "blockbuster" status.

-Action & Sports: High sales, moderate scores.

-Role-Playing & Strategy: Higher average scores, but lower mass-market sales.
